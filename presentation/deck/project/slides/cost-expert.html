The cost expert is the one that changes code. It begins with the bill: where the tokens go, by node and by model, and every node that carries more than a twentieth of them has to be accounted for in its report. Seven skills say where waste hides and how to fix it for each provider. It keeps the graph's structure as it is; a change to the structure is the design expert's to report. When a project has nothing to check its agent against, it may call the tester.
