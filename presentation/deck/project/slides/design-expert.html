Work in progress

The design expert is work in progress. It asks a different question from the cost expert: is this the right design at all. It runs on a stronger model because that is where judgement pays. It works from a tool called shape, which counts what the graph declared against what it actually did, for the graph and for each graph nested in it. Four skills: whether a step needs a model at all; fourteen patterns, each with when it is warranted and what the simpler design is; work that nobody waits for; and dated facts about LangGraph. It reports findings in two sizes, small and redesign, and it does not change code.
