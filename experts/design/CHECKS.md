For every node that carries more than a twentieth of the tokens:

- needs-a-model: whether this step needs a model at all: how its replies vary across requests that differ, and whether anything reads them.
- pattern: which pattern it is part of, by what ran, and the number that shows it.
- warranted: whether that pattern earns its keep here: `fine` with the number, or `cut` with the simpler design.
- failures: what raised or was swallowed there, or the count that says nothing did.
